1. Install and connect Swiggy

  1. Install CraveLens from the Chrome Web Store, then pin it using Chrome’s Extensions menu.
  2. Open the popup. Keep Server environment on Prod for normal use. Dev is for a separately configured local development server.
  3. Click Connect Swiggy and complete authorization in the tab that opens. Return to the popup once connected.
  4. Select a saved Delivery address. Check the label and address before preparing a cart. If you allow location access, CraveLens can select the nearest saved address; that comparison happens locally.
  5. Turn on CraveLens, configure your models and preferences below, then click Save.

You need a supported Swiggy account, a serviceable saved address and a Chrome device capable of running your chosen local vision model. Connecting authorizes cart preparation; it does not authorize placing an order.

2. Find food in videos

Open a YouTube video or Short, an Instagram Reel, or a Facebook Watch/video post. Enable that site under the popup’s auto-detection controls, turn on CraveLens and let the video play. Scheduled scans use a small local food detector before asking your local vision model to identify the dish.

  • Food sensitivity: adjust the detector threshold when scenes are missed or too many candidates are being checked. It does not bypass the final food-verification confidence check.
  • Model scan interval: increase it to reduce scanning work or decrease it to sample more often.
  • Per-site controls: disable automatic scanning on individual supported services while retaining manual scans.
  • Enable switch: pause CraveLens when you do not want it preparing suggestions.

Only a valid local food identification with sufficient confidence proceeds. The extension remembers confirmed dishes and scene signatures to suppress duplicates. A quiet scene, an uncertain identification, or a repeat may produce no visible response.

3. Scan a frame or select an area

Use Scan current frame or the default keyboard shortcut Ctrl + Shift + Y on a supported video page. A manual frame scan goes directly to the local vision model instead of waiting for the scheduled detector gate.

On another normal webpage, use the shortcut and drag a rectangle around the food you want identified. Only that selected region is analyzed. Under Keyboard shortcut behavior, choose Auto for video-frame scans on supported sites and lasso elsewhere, or Lasso to always select an area.

If Chrome has assigned a different shortcut or another extension uses it, check chrome://extensions/shortcuts. Chrome’s protected pages, including its settings pages, do not permit ordinary page capture.

4. Personalize your cart

In Food preferences and routines, describe the constraints you want the cart model to consider: vegetarian meals, preferred cuisine, spice level, portion needs, budget, or routines tied to a day or time. Save your changes before the next scan.

Automatic preferences on your first cart

If Food preferences and routines is empty, CraveLens automatically reads verified order history for your selected delivery address before preparing a cart. It saves tentative preferences and routines, when supported by the actual history, and reuses that profile for up to 30 days. No verified history means no invented preferences; that check is repeated after a day. Existing preferences you enter take priority. A failed history or model step stops preparation before cart edits: check your Swiggy connection, address and model, then scan again.

After a completed cart, Saved inferred preferences appears below the empty editor for that address, with a tentative label, order coverage and update date. Expand History & evidence to read its supporting notes. Suggested preferences use the same layout, with order coverage and a Not saved yet label; Use suggestion accepts the draft and Keep original leaves your preferences unchanged. Review these patterns and enter and save your own constraints to override them. Disconnecting or reconnecting Swiggy clears the saved server profile. Keep Chrome running for local inference; the selected cart model and hosted-fallback policy also apply to automatic generation.

Build my preferences from order history

  1. Connect Swiggy, select a saved delivery address and configure your cart reasoning model. Write any preferences you already know, or leave the box empty.
  2. Click the sparkle inside Food preferences and routines. The agent requests 15 recent orders per history call (including active and past orders), verifies each order’s delivery address ID, and uses the latest seven matching orders to draft a refined preference prompt when available. Orders from other addresses or with unverifiable details are excluded.
  3. Follow the live progress beneath the editor. A rotating glow indicates work in progress. If the local model fails, your saved hosted-fallback policy applies; approve in the popup or linked Telegram chat when asked.
  4. Review Suggested preferences and its evidence notes against your original text. Check allergies, dietary restrictions, budgets and routines. Past purchases can suggest tentative preferences, but do not establish medical or dietary requirements.
  5. Choose Use suggestion to save the refined prompt, or Keep original to discard it without replacing your text.

This action only reads history and drafts text; it does not change a cart or order food. Keep Chrome running for local AI. You can reopen the popup to recover an active run or draft while its ten-minute retention window remains valid. Runs can fail if the account, model or server is unavailable. Swiggy may expose fewer than seven matching orders; progress and evidence notes report how many were verified and any coverage limits. With no verified order history for the selected address, your original preferences are returned unchanged without generating new inferred preferences.

The cart model receives your explicit preferences, the identified dish, dining context and observation time. It is instructed to consult relevant Swiggy order history, but fresh history retrieval inside the cart loop is not enforced on every run. Automatic first-cart generation reads verified history when there is no saved profile and your explicit preferences are empty. A preference inferred from past orders can only inform an explanation when that evidence was actually retrieved.

Visual identification and menu descriptions can be incomplete. Review the final item, variant and add-ons, particularly for allergies and dietary restrictions. A description in your preferences is guidance for cart preparation, not a restaurant guarantee.

5. Choose your AI models

Frame verification model

Under VLM configuration, choose Auto · browser-local first, Gemini Nano, Gemma 3n, or an installed Ollama vision model. Availability depends on your browser, hardware and installed models. Frames and selected page regions stay on your device.

Gemma 3n downloads into the browser after saving. Keep the browser open, watch the download indicator and wait for readiness before scanning. You can cancel a download or remove a cached VLM from the popup. Gemini Nano requires compatible Chrome support. For Ollama, enter the local host, press Test, grant the requested host permission if necessary and select a discovered vision-capable model. Follow Local AI setup in the popup if the connection needs configuration.

Cart reasoning model

Under Agentic orchestration, choose browser-local LiteRT, local Ollama, Google Gemini, or an OpenAI-compatible provider. Local cart inference still uses the CraveLens server and Swiggy for orchestration and shopping data; it is not an entirely offline ordering flow.

LiteRT offers Gemma 4 E2B, E4B, 12B, 26B A4B and 31B. Model downloads range from roughly 1.9 GB to 17.9 GB. A listed model may exceed your device’s available memory; downloaded bytes do not guarantee it can initialize. Start with a smaller model, keep Chrome running and check readiness. The popup lets you cancel downloads or remove cached models.

For local cart models, Context length controls how much context is retained and affects memory use. Thinking mode requests extra reasoning where supported and can increase latency. For hosted providers, the deployment’s configured default can be used with blank fields for its matching provider. A different hosted provider may require your own model, API key and compatible base URL. Keys are write-only in the popup and stored encrypted by the server.

6. Set local-model failure behavior

Under When the local cart model fails, choose one option and Save:

Option What happens
Automatically use a hosted model Eligible LiteRT or Ollama cart-inference failures switch to the configured hosted provider without another permission prompt.
Ask before using a hosted model The default. You approve or reject the switch. In Telegram mode, the request arrives in your linked chat.
Stop without switching models The local error is reported and preparation stops.

Hosted fallback sends the run’s model context to that provider. It requires a configured hosted model and does not upload video frames. This setting applies to cart reasoning, not a hosted replacement for failed local image identification. Cancellation and an unverified cart do not trigger a hosted retry. Stopping does not undo cart edits already made; check Swiggy before retrying a failed preparation.

7. Choose how your cart arrives

Open Cart experience, select While you watch, and Save. All three modes can prepare a cart; every mode requires a separate confirmation to order.

Experience How to use it
Show progress on screen Follow preparation on the page, then review the receipt and the source-aware cart shelf.
Notify me when my cart is ready Preparation runs without progress overlays. Click the small cart-ready nudge to open the regular receipt, or dismiss the nudge.
Send my cart to Telegram Receive the prepared receipt in a verified private bot chat with explanation and checkout buttons. Connect Telegram first.

CraveLens permits one active cart per extension connection. Another cart waits until the current reservation is released or expires. Different devices have separate connections; avoid simultaneously preparing carts for the same Swiggy account on several devices. Keep the browser running for local AI, even when the final receipt goes to Telegram.

8. Connect and use Telegram

Telegram delivery must be enabled by the CraveLens service operator. If the popup reports that Telegram is unavailable, you cannot enable it by entering a username. For self-hosted deployments, the operator setup is documented in the repository’s Telegram setup guide.

  1. In the popup’s Cart experience section, click Connect Telegram.
  2. Open the bot link in Telegram and press Start in a private chat. The link is single-use and expires after five minutes; request another link if needed.
  3. Reopen the extension and check the displayed account. Click Confirm this Telegram account only when it is yours.
  4. Select Send my cart to Telegram and Save. Below the account connection controls, enable Silent Telegram messages to deliver without a notification sound. It is off by default.
  5. When a cart arrives, an available product photo appears above a formatted receipt with clear restaurant, item, amount and delivery sections. If the image cannot be sent, you still receive the receipt. Longer receipts may span messages, with checkout buttons on the final one. Review its restaurant, items, delivery address, payable amount, delivery estimate and expiry. Use Why this cart? for the explanation or Dismiss to reject the cart.
  6. Select an offered payment method, then use the separate confirmation button to proceed. Selecting the method alone does not place an order.

UPI checkout sends a payment link when available and a QR image. The server checks payment status; Check payment requests a manual refresh. Cancel payment process rechecks the live status and stops CraveLens tracking while still pending. It does not cancel or refund a transfer in your UPI app. A paid or uncertain result must be reviewed in Swiggy. Treat any uncertain payment result as a reason to check Swiggy before trying again.

Your linked name appears prominently in the account card. Use Disconnect account in the popup to revoke old bot-button access and return to the on-screen experience. Telegram receives your cart, selected delivery address, explanations you request and payment status or UPI requests. It does not receive video frames. Blocking the bot prevents delivery; expired carts and stale buttons cannot be used to confirm an order.

9. Review, customize and understand your cart

The browser receipt shows the verified restaurant, line items, quantities, savings, fees, taxes and final payable amount. Check the selected address and delivery estimate. The cart shelf keeps prepared suggestions associated with their source in the current tab.

Use the receipt’s follow-up instruction field to ask for changes, browse or search the restaurant menu, adjust quantities, add or remove items, or apply an eligible coupon when those controls are available. Changes refresh the verified receipt; review the new amount before confirming. For a Telegram-delivered cart, send a follow-up request of up to 500 characters in the private bot chat, or reply to the receipt/product photo. CraveLens uses the current cart and conversation to prepare and verify your changes, then sends a refreshed receipt for a new review and confirmation. Expired carts and carts in payment or checkout cannot be edited. Old bot buttons cannot authorize a changed cart.

Why this cart? presents spaced bullet points explaining the recorded selection evidence: relevance to the detected dish, explicit preferences, order history when consulted, menu availability and verified pricing or discounts. Local and hosted cart models both use personalized explanations. If explanation generation fails, an evidence-based explanation is returned. It summarizes the decision evidence, not the model’s hidden internal reasoning.

Coupons depend on Swiggy’s live eligibility and may be limited to a payment method. No available coupon means no guaranteed coupon savings. Prepared suggestions normally expire after ten minutes; create a fresh cart rather than trying to use an expired receipt.

10. Confirm and pay

  1. Review the final receipt, delivery address and total.
  2. Choose one of the payment methods Swiggy currently offers for that cart.
  3. Press the explicit confirmation control. The server rechecks the actual cart and amount before submitting.
  • Cash on Delivery: confirm the order and pay according to Swiggy’s COD flow.
  • UPI: use the Swiggy payment link or scan the QR with your UPI app. Complete payment there. CraveLens checks the payment result before finalizing a paid order. Browser checkout also offers status and pending-payment controls where available.
  • Swiggy Money: appears only when Swiggy explicitly reports it available for the cart. Confirmation submits wallet checkout; there is no UPI QR step.

Availability depends on your account and cart. CraveLens checks the Builders Club ₹1,000 payable limit before ordering. A changed cart requires a fresh review. Neither automatic detection, receipt delivery nor selecting a payment method places an order.

When placement or payment has an uncertain result, check your Swiggy cart, orders and payment activity before repeating the flow. CraveLens does not automatically retry order placement. An uncertain checkout remains held for review rather than submitting a second order.

Multiple dishes in one scan

A video frame or lasso selection can identify up to eight main prepared dishes in focus, excluding garnishes, condiments, small side accompaniments, drinks and background food, each with separate confidence and ingredient evidence. Missing cuisine labels are treated as unknown and do not prevent the scan from completing. The cart agent considers the list, your preferences and live availability to prepare one cart from one restaurant. Menu and restaurant searches have no per-tool quotas; cart planning has no cumulative model-call or graph-step limit. The cart loop still has a three-minute deadline and stops repeated invalid or missing tool calls. It may omit uncertain dishes or suggest alternatives; review the receipt rather than assuming everything in the image was added. Frames and selected images stay on-device; the structured dish list reaches the service and the selected cart model.

11. Troubleshoot and manage privacy

No food prompt or cart

Check that CraveLens is enabled, your site’s auto-detection is on, the video is playing, Swiggy is connected and a saved address is selected. Verify that the local vision model is ready. Try a manual scan of a clear food frame. Duplicate scenes, low-confidence identifications and an existing active cart can suppress another suggestion. In quiet modes, progress overlays are intentionally absent.

Preparing cart disappears

If another cart is active, the on-screen flow shows Cart preparation paused. If the cart is ready for review, click Review existing cart to reopen its receipt on this page, then review or reject it before scanning again. If preparation or payment is still active, wait for it to finish or check its payment status in Swiggy. A manual selection shows the outcome even in a quiet mode. If live progress cannot connect, preparation still runs and its final result or error appears when available. Automatic quiet scans record unsuccessful outcomes in debug state and the browser console.

Build my preferences fails

Select a saved delivery address before clicking the sparkle: Swiggy requires it to read order history. If history retrieval fails, check the selected address and reconnect Swiggy. Preference building reads recent account orders without checking their saved delivery-address IDs, so orders from other addresses may contribute. Structured details and Swiggy text receipts are supported. If no details can be read or order IDs do not match, enter preferences manually; no suggestion is generated. Automatic cart preparation continues without inferred preferences when history cannot be verified. If model generation or draft validation fails, check the cart model and fallback settings, keep Chrome running for local inference, or try another model. The popup identifies the failed stage; your original preferences stay unchanged.

Local model offline, slow or out of memory

Keep Chrome running and wait for downloads to finish. Try a smaller LiteRT model, reduce context length, or test your Ollama host. The configured fallback policy applies only when a compatible hosted provider is available. Selecting a local model does not make shopping work without a server connection.

Telegram link, delivery or buttons fail

Request a new link if it expired; press Start in the private bot chat and confirm the account in the popup. Make sure the bot is not blocked and the cart has not expired. A changed receipt, disconnected account or stale callback requires a fresh review. If the bot is unavailable, choose an on-screen mode and contact the service operator.

Find your extension version

The version appears below the links at the bottom of the popup. It reflects the installed extension; include it when reporting a problem.

Debug and disconnect

Enable Debug overlay to inspect detection boxes, confidence, local verification results, timings and errors. The VLM section uses a compact panel and shows one identified dish at a time in a scrollable list that shrinks for a single result, with separate confidence, cuisine, description and visible ingredients; uncertain entries are marked. Turn it off again for a quiet viewing experience. Use the popup’s connection controls to disconnect Swiggy or Telegram when you no longer want them linked.

Frames and lasso selections stay local. The service receives structured dish and source information for cart preparation; Swiggy supplies account and shopping data. Hosted cart models and optional Telegram delivery receive the context needed for those features. When service diagnostics are enabled, the operator can correlate cart preparation and checkout using opaque identifiers and metadata such as tool names, timings and outcome codes. Screenshots, preferences, history, prompts, model responses and payment payloads are excluded from these diagnostics. The service also counts registrations, confirmed orders and dish labels already submitted for cart preparation for aggregate operational reporting. Browser-only scans are not added to these server counters. Read the privacy policy for storage and sharing details, or contact support with the error and the steps that caused it.

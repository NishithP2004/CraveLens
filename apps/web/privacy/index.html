Privacy Policy

CraveLens uses the minimum data needed to recognize food in YouTube videos and prepare a Swiggy cart that you can review. Food-frame analysis happens on your device, and an order is never placed without your explicit confirmation.

1. Scope

This policy describes how the CraveLens Chrome extension, landing site, and supporting CraveLens service (together, “CraveLens,” “we,” or “us”) handle information. It applies when you use the extension on YouTube, connect a Swiggy account, ask CraveLens to prepare a cart, or contact us.

2. Data we process

Video and website context

The extension accesses the active YouTube video so it can sample frames, read the video identifier and title, and record the playback timestamp where food was detected. Sampled frames are processed locally in an offscreen extension document by the bundled food detector and a locally loaded Gemma model. Frames are not sent to our server, Swiggy, or the provider of the cart-planning AI model.

When food is confidently identified, CraveLens sends our supporting service the YouTube video identifier, title, playback timestamp, detector confidence, and a structured food description such as the dish, cuisine, likely ingredients, confidence, and dining context.

Swiggy account and delivery information

If you choose to connect Swiggy, we process an OAuth session identifier and authorization tokens needed to maintain that connection. Through Swiggy, CraveLens may retrieve saved delivery addresses (which can contain a name, address, phone number, label, and coordinates), recent food orders, restaurant and menu information, cart contents, prices, fees, discounts, delivery estimates, and available payment-method names. CraveLens does not ask for or store your payment-card number, CVV, Swiggy password, or PIN.

Approximate or precise location

If saved Swiggy addresses include coordinates and you grant Chrome’s geolocation permission, the extension reads your device coordinates to select the nearest saved address. This distance comparison happens locally in the extension. Your live device coordinates are not sent to our server or Swiggy by CraveLens.

Settings and extension activity

The extension stores settings such as enabled status, detection sensitivity, debug preference, selected address identifier and label, connection session identifier and expiration, detected dishes, and prepared-cart history. It also handles progress events needed to show what the cart agent is doing. We do not monitor keystrokes, mouse movement, general browsing history, personal communications, or activity on sites other than the YouTube pages covered by the extension.

3. How we use data

We use the information above only to provide and improve CraveLens’ user-facing purpose:

  • detect and identify visible food in the current YouTube video;
  • avoid duplicate suggestions for the same dish or video moment;
  • connect the Swiggy account you authorize and select a delivery address;
  • use relevant order history and preferences to find a suitable restaurant and menu item;
  • prepare, verify, and display a cart, charges, delivery estimate, and applicable discount;
  • place an order only after your explicit confirmation;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot, and prevent misuse of the service; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not use or transfer user data for personalized advertising, unrelated profiling, lending, or determining creditworthiness.

4. When data is shared

We do not sell user data. We disclose data only as needed for the following approved purposes:

  • Swiggy: to authenticate the connection you request and retrieve addresses, orders, restaurants, menus, carts, offers, and order results.
  • Cart-planning AI provider: to process the identified food description, selected-address summary, and relevant Swiggy results so the agent can choose and verify a personalized cart. Video frames and live device coordinates are not provided to this service.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: to operate and secure the supporting service and its database.
  • Legal or safety disclosures: if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, CraveLens, or the public.
  • Business transfer: as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale, subject to this policy and applicable law.

These providers may process information only for the service being supplied and under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. Storage and retention

Extension settings, session identifiers, detected-food history, and prepared-cart history are stored locally in your Chrome profile or the YouTube site storage used by the extension. You can remove this local information by clearing extension/site data or uninstalling CraveLens.

The supporting service stores OAuth credentials in access-restricted server storage and may retain detection records and prepared-cart records in its database. We retain information only while it is reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain security and records, resolve disputes, or satisfy legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by data type and operational need. You may request deletion using the contact details below.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Disable food detection from the extension popup.
  • Deny geolocation; you can still select a saved address manually or use Swiggy’s first recommended address.
  • Choose which saved delivery address CraveLens uses.
  • Decline any proposed cart. No order is placed without your confirmation.
  • Revoke CraveLens’ Swiggy authorization through the available Swiggy account controls.
  • Clear extension and site data or uninstall the extension.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable law.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including OAuth authorization rather than collecting a Swiggy password and access-restricted storage for server-side credentials. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

CraveLens is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CraveLens changes or as required by law. We will post the revised policy here and update the “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects how we use previously collected personal information, we will provide additional notice where required.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email me@nishithp.page. To help us respond, include “CraveLens privacy request” in the subject line and describe your request.

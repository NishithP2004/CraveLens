A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Still curious?

Does CraveLens place orders automatically?＋

Never. CraveLens can prepare the perfect cart, but the final order always waits for your explicit confirmation.

Which videos does it work with?＋

CraveLens currently works on YouTube videos in Chrome and recognizes a growing catalog of dishes and food moments.

Is my browsing data uploaded?＋

No. The first food-detection step happens locally in your browser, and only a chosen food frame is used to identify the dish.

Do I need to connect Swiggy?＋

You can explore detection without ordering, but connecting Swiggy is needed to use saved addresses, nearby listings, menus, offers, carts, and checkout.

Are prices and offers final?＋

CraveLens shows Swiggy listing, price, fee, delivery, and offer information as provided at the time it prepares your cart. Always review the final Swiggy checkout details before confirming.

Can it handle allergies or dietary preferences?＋

CraveLens can use preferences you enter, like vegetarian choices or allergy warnings, to guide recommendations. You should still verify item details with Swiggy and the restaurant before ordering.

Which local AI options are supported?＋

CraveLens can use the configured local VLM path available in your browser setup, including Gemini Nano, browser-downloaded Gemma 3n, or an Ollama vision model where supported.

Does CraveLens store my payment details?＋

No. CraveLens does not ask for or store your card number, CVV, Swiggy password, UPI ID, or UPI PIN. Payment flows are handled through Swiggy.

Who helps if an order goes wrong?＋

Order support, delivery issues, cancellations, and refunds are handled through Swiggy's customer-support process, except where the issue is caused by CraveLens itself.

Where can I read the legal details?＋

The short version lives here, and the full details are available in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
